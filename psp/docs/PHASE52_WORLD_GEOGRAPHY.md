# Phase 52 world geography layout

The Emberwake trail is a small eastward valley shaped by water and rising
ground. Hearth Clearing sits on its sheltered western edge. The route follows
the land instead of treating each region as a separate destination.

```text
                         THE STILLWARD
                              |
                       GLASSWAKE EXPANSE
                              |
                        HOLLOWSTONE CAVE
                         /             \\
                 FERNVEIL WOODS --- NORTHWOOD REACH
                      |                    |
                 SUNTHREAD MARSH ---- LANTERN REST
                      |
                 HEARTH CLEARING
                      |
                 WAYFARER LODGE
```

## Landforms and routes

| Area | Physical form | Connections and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hearth Clearing | A dry, sheltered clearing at the west end of a low valley. | The Fernveil Road follows firm ground east. Wayfarer Lodge sits inside the settlement, beside the road. |
| Fernveil Woods | A broad old forest on the valley's eastern side. | Its southern edge drains into Sunthread Marsh. Its northern ridge trail climbs toward Northwood. A natural limestone fault descends into Hollowstone. |
| Sunthread Marsh | A low wetland fed by the forest's southern streams. | Raised boardwalks travel east from Fernveil to Lantern Rest, where the ground rises out of the reed beds. Water and deep mud prevent a direct shortcut to the northern ridge. |
| Lantern Rest | A small safe settlement on a stone shelf above the marsh. | It is supplied by the boardwalk and is the practical southern approach to Northwood Reach. |
| Northwood Reach | Cold, elevated pine country above Lantern Rest and northern Fernveil. | Switchback paths meet at a wind-worn saddle. The saddle exposes the cave mouth; unstable cliffs keep the route intentionally narrow. |
| Hollowstone Cave | A limestone passage underneath the saddle between the forest and highlands. | It exists because groundwater cut through the uplifted stone. The only reliable eastern exit leads into Glasswake, so it is the natural midgame passage. |
| Glasswake Expanse | A dry basin east of the ridge, exposed to altered Vey currents. | The cave opens onto its western rim. A single old causeway leads onward to The Stillward. Sheer basin walls block travel around the cave. |
| The Stillward | A raised ancient site at the basin's eastern head. | It is deliberately isolated: the Glasswake causeway is its sole ordinary approach. |

## Geographic rules

- Streams leave Fernveil southward, spread into Sunthread, and pass Lantern
  Rest before leaving the playable valley. This gives the marsh a clear source
  and puts the safe settlement on stable high ground.
- Elevation rises from Hearth through Fernveil to Northwood, then drops below
  ground through Hollowstone before opening into Glasswake's enclosed basin.
- The road network has two believable approaches to Northwood: Lantern's
  switchback and Fernveil's northern ridge trail. Both meet before the cave
  entrance, so the guardian can protect one meaningful choke point.
- The cave is not a shortcut: the forest-side fissure is unsuitable for public
  travel. The accessible entrance is the Northwood saddle, and the east exit
  is the route required to continue the story.

## World-map representation

The in-game overview now follows this route. Existing selectable locations
retain their save-compatible IDs. Northwood Reach, Glasswake Expanse, and The
Stillward are deliberately displayed as planned landmarks only: they have no
new map IDs, cursor targets, warp links, or encounters until their individual
map phases begin.

Detailed tiles, collision, exits, and local map layouts remain deferred.
