relationship to a place, a specific stake in the Quieting, and a job in the
player's route; no separate villain faction is needed.

## Supporting locals

Sen, Varel, Ela, and other existing map residents remain useful local voices.
They are not required at every major beat. They provide habitat detail,
gate-state dialogue, and a sense that each settlement has people living there
beyond the story cast.

## Cast rules

- Each core character returns only when their place or knowledge matters.
- Disagreement comes from different responsibilities—access, safety, care, and
  limited information—not from manufactured betrayal.
- The player remains the active investigator; recurring characters offer
  context, challenge, and consequence without solving the journey for them.
